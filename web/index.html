How to read this

Each third-party game has a candidate exit at the wave nearest its 12, 18, 24 and 36 month anniversary. The starting odds are how often games have left at that anniversary on the sheet. A listing on Pure Xbox's monthly forecast raises them, missing from a month that has a forecast lowers them, and a place in the Premium catalogue lowers them too.

Bands: Likely is 45% or more, Possible is 25 to 44%, Thin is under 25%. Microsoft, Bethesda and Activision Blizzard games are assumed to stay. EA Play and Retro Classics games are left out.

Urgency: Too late means the hours don't fit before the wave even if you start today. Start now means the start-by date has arrived.
