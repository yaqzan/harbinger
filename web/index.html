    How to read this

    Every game here is one you can only play through one subscription, unless it is dimmed. A dimmed game is one you own, or one the other service also has, so its exit costs you nothing. The tag says where you'd keep playing.

    Each third-party Game Pass game has a candidate exit at the wave nearest its 12, 18, 24 and 36 month anniversary. The starting odds are how often games have left at that anniversary. A listing on Pure Xbox's monthly forecast raises them, missing from a month that has a forecast lowers them, and a place in the Premium catalogue lowers them too. PS Plus games leave on the third Monday of the month and use the same anniversaries with no forecast.

    Bands: Likely is 45% or more, Possible is 25 to 44%, Thin is under 25%. Microsoft, Bethesda and Activision Blizzard games are assumed to stay. EA Play and Retro Classics games are left out.

    Odds are a model estimate and can be wrong. Completion hours come from the sheets and nobody has checked them. Game Pass means Ultimate on an Xbox console; PC-only games are left out.
